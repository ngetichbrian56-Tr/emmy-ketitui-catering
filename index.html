About us

Cooking with care, serving with pride

With more than 10 years of experience, Emmy Ketitui Catering Services is a trusted catering business based in Ketitui, Kiptagich Ward, Kuresoi South, serving events across Nakuru County.

We are a hands-on team dedicated to making every event memorable through flavourful food and dependable service. We cook fresh, serve beautifully and handle the details so you can enjoy your day.

  • Freshly prepared meals, cooked to order
  • Complete service: food, crockery, cutlery and serving
  • Professional, neat and punctual team
  • Menus tailored to your guests and budget

Good to know

Questions People Ask

How early should I book?

For weddings and large events, book as early as you can. For smaller events, call us to check the date.

Do you serve outside Ketitui?

Yes. We serve events across Nakuru County. Tell us your venue and we will confirm.

Do you bring plates, cutlery and staff?

Yes. We bring chafing dishes, plates, cutlery and a uniformed serving team on request.

Can the menu be changed?

Yes. We build menus around your guests, budget and any dietary needs.

How much does catering cost?

It depends on the number of guests, the menu you choose and the level of service. Tell us your date, venue and guest count and we will give you a free quote with no obligation.

How do I pay?

We agree the price when you request a quote. Ask Emmy about deposits and payment methods.

Planning an event? Tell us the date and number of guests and we will send you a quote.
